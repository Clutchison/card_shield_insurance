# Beneficiaries: can a carrier or will-writing partner back the list?

**Date:** 2026-11-02. **Question from grill session 1:** could the underwriting carrier supply a bespoke will-writing service, giving more validity to the beneficiaries a User lists?

Confidence tags: **[P]** primary source, **[S]** secondary, **[U]** unverified.

## Short answer

- **A carrier almost certainly won't provide one.** Every insurer bundle found is a life insurer (Haven Life, USAA Life, Ethos, MetLife), usually through Trust & Will. No P&C or collectibles carrier (Chubb, Markel, American Collectors, WAX) offers will documents.
- **A partner wouldn't add validity anyway.** A list is valid when the will is properly executed, refers to the list, and is in a state that allows such lists. It doesn't matter who drafts the will.
- **Realistic shape:** Card Shield contracts with Trust & Will, or a similar provider, through its affiliate or free/discounted-plan program. The will includes a separate-writing clause, and the app exports a signed, dated list of slabs with cert numbers. Card Shield or its agency pays, not the carrier.

## 1. Insurers that bundle will or estate services

- **Haven Life (MassMutual):** gives Haven Term policyholders a free Trust & Will trust, stated as worth $599 or $699 depending on the page [P, [Trust & Will](https://trustandwill.com/learn/haven-life-partnership)].
- **USAA Life:** members get discounted Trust & Will plans [S, [The Wealth Advisor](https://www.thewealthadvisor.com/article/usaa-life-insurance-announces-new-relationship-trust-will)].
- **Ethos:** free will with eligible policies, through its own subsidiary Ethos Estate Planning LLC, which says it is "not a law firm." It is excluded in WA, SD, AK and LA [P, [Ethos](https://www.ethos.com/get-a-free-will/)].
- **MetLife:** includes will preparation in group legal plans and with some group life coverage [P, [MetLife](https://www.metlife.com/MWBwelcome/will-prep/)].
- **Erie (P&C):** invested in Trust & Will in 2024. The announcement doesn't say Erie offers it to policyholders [P, [Trust & Will](https://trustandwill.com/learn/trust-and-will-announces-investment-from-erie-strategic-ventures)].
- **Chubb:** collector services include estate valuation and "legacy planning" consultation for select clients. These are not will documents [S, [IA Magazine, 2015](https://www.iamagazine.com/markets/read/2015/01/12/chubb-releases-new-service-for-collectors)].

## 2. Will providers a startup could integrate

- **Trust & Will:** offers partnerships as free or discounted plans, a policy rider, employee benefits, or an affiliate program [P, [partnerships](https://trustandwill.com/partnerships)]. There's no public API and integrations go through sales [S]. Retail is about $199 for a will and $499 for a trust [S].
- **FreeWill:** free to consumers and funded by nonprofits. It's a poor fit for a for-profit startup [S].
- **LegalZoom, Gentreo, Willful:** no published partner terms found [U].
- **No provider has been confirmed** to support a tangible-personal-property memorandum. Ask each one directly [U].

## 3. Using an app-generated list as a separate writing

- Under UPC §2-513, a will can refer to a list of tangible items that exists at death and can be changed after the will is signed. The list must be signed and describe items and recipients with "reasonable certainty." It can't pass money [P, [Utah §75-2-513](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title75/Chapter2/C75-2-S513_1800010118000101.html)]. A signed, dated export with cert numbers looks like it would qualify. Whether an e-signature counts as "signed" is untested [U].
- **States with no general separate-writing statute:** CT, LA, DC, NH, NY, RI, TX, VT, WV [S, [ezel.ai](https://ezel.ai/surveys/personal-property-memorandum-requirements)].
- **States that only allow fixed incorporation by reference** (changes need full will formalities): AL, IL, KY, MD, MS, NC, OH, OK [S, same].
- **California (Prob. Code §6132):** caps the list at $5,000 per item and $25,000 in total, which matters for high-value slabs [S, same; verify the statute text, U].

## 4. Unauthorized practice of law (UPL)

- **North Carolina, G.S. 84-2.2:** document-generating websites are exempt only if templates are reviewed by an NC attorney, disclaimers are shown, and the provider registers annually with the State Bar [P, [NCGA](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_84/GS_84-2.2.html)].
- **Texas, Gov't Code §81.101(c):** software is exempt if it "clearly and conspicuously" says it is not a substitute for an attorney [P, [HB 1507](https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/76R/billtext/html/HB01507F.htm)].
- **Low risk:** a neutral inventory or wish-list export.
- **Higher risk:** the app choosing clauses, advising on validity, or generating a will. That's an argument for partnering with a licensed provider rather than building it.
- **Insurance-side risk:** a free will bundled with a policy could count as an illegal inducement or rebate under state insurance law. Check state by state [U].
