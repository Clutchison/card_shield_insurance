# Card store and table vendor insurance, and store inventory software

**Date:** 2026-11-02. **Question from grill session 1:** what do Card stores and Table vendors buy for insurance today, what does it cost, and is inventory tracking already served?

Confidence tags: **[P]** primary source, **[S]** secondary, **[U]** unverified. **EST** = estimate.

## Summary

- **Inventory software is crowded, but slab-level tracking isn't.** Store tools ($99–150/month) are built around TCG singles. Few track individual slabs with a value. That's the record CIS requires dealers to keep, so the inventory product can directly support an insurance claim.
- **Dealer coverage exists** (CIS, others). Evidence that it's hard to get is anecdotal. Confirm the gap through broker and wholesaler interviews before building on it.
- **Commission is thin:** about 15% of a $500–2,500 premium is roughly **$75–375 per account per year**. Most store revenue would have to come from the software subscription, with insurance as the retention hook.
- **The market is small:** about 1,500–3,000 US card stores (EST) plus thousands of Table vendors. Table vendors are most exposed to unattended-vehicle and transit exclusions, which is a clear hook for them.

## 1. What dealers buy, and who writes it

- **Typical structure [U]:** a BOP or GL/property policy for the premises and liability, plus an inland-marine dealer's-stock floater for the inventory (market value, shows, transit, consignment). Jewelers-block policies are the closest model.
- **CIS dealer policy** [P, [collectinsure.com/dealers](https://collectinsure.com/dealers/)]:
  - Covers storefront, online and show dealers, at market value less a $500 deductible. An older page says $200 [S].
  - Covers US and Canada shows and travel, "including unattended auto and checked baggage," worldwide shipments, documented consignment and scheduled storage.
  - No schedule or appraisal needed, but **the dealer must keep their own inventory records**.
  - Carrier unnamed (AM Best "A" group). Limits and price not published.
- **American Collectors:** collector-side products only; no dealer program found [S].
- **Thimble:** per-event general liability, $1M per occurrence, up to 5 days. **Liability only, no inventory.** Some pages cap events at 250 attendees [P/S, [Thimble](https://www.thimble.com/event-insurance/trade-shows)].
- Not found: Hobby Insurance, or Hartford, Hiscox or Next appetite for card dealers.

## 2. Price points

- **Dealer:** about $500–1,000 per year for $100k of stock, and about $2,300 per year for $500k. Inventory covered on-site at The National [S, [Sports Collectors Digest, Jan 2021](https://sportscollectorsdigest.com/news/are-your-sports-collectibles-insured), dated].
- **Collector:** about $350–550 per year per $100k [S, same].
- **Implied dealer rate:** EST 0.5–1.0% of insured stock per year.
- **Exclusions:**
  - CIS collector forms exclude mysterious disappearance [P, [brochure](https://collectinsure.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2021/05//Collector-Brochure.pdf)].
  - Jewelers-block style forms strictly exclude theft from an unattended vehicle. NY courts upheld this in 2023 and 2026 (*Avi & Co.*) [P, [NY Courts](https://www.nycourts.gov/reporter/3dseries/2026/2026_00186.htm)].
  - Standard commercial policies may pay cost, not market value [S].

## 3. Evidence of a coverage gap (anecdotal)

- One shop owner "could find only one company willing to insure his shop's merchandise" [S, [CNN via KSL, 2026-04-04](https://www.ksl.com/article/51477273/pokmon-cards-are-igniting-an-international-crime-spree)].
- About 8 Minnesota and Wisconsin stores were robbed in two months; the article calls it an "epidemic" [S, [SCD, 2026-10-02](https://sportscollectorsdigest.com/rash-of-robberies-break-ins-plague-hobby-card-shops)].
- A Salem, OR shop lost $75k in June 2026 [S, [KPTV](https://www.kptv.com/2026/06/03/thief-walks-out-salem-store-with-75000-stolen-trading-cards/)].
- Travelers denied a $250k claim from Mamba Cards after a 2022 burglary [S, [The Blast](https://theblast.com/458246/high-end-sports-card-shop-sues-over-insane-smash-grab/)].
- Claims that insurers refuse or surcharge Pokémon shops are unsourced [U].
- **No carrier statements, non-renewal data or trade surveys were found.**

## 4. Producer commissions

- Inland marine averages 15.7% (range 7.5–25.3%) [S, [Compensation Resources, 2025](https://www.compensationresources.com/insurance-producer-compensation-0825)].
- MICO/Encova schedule: BOP 15–20%, commercial inland marine 15% [P, [Encova](https://encova.com/globalassets/encova-site/corporate-communications/exhibit-c---commission-rates-updated-6.10.22.pdf)].
- EST: about $75–150 commission per year on a $500–1,000 dealer premium.

## 5. Size of the target

- Card Shop Finder lists 1,734 shops as of 2026-10-08; it's mainly a TCG directory and still incomplete [S, [Card Shop Finder](https://thecardshopfinder.com/guides/finding-card-shops/opening-a-card-shop/)].
- Sports-only shops fell from about 10,000 at peak to "several hundred," according to a Topps executive [S, [Crain's Detroit](https://prod.crainsdetroit.com/node/689456)].
- The National 2026 had 600+ dealers and sold out its booths [S, [Card Shop Finder](https://thecardshopfinder.com/blog/national-2026-whats-new/), [NSCC](https://nsccshow.com/dealers)].
- EST: 1,500–3,000 US card stores if TCG stores are counted; far fewer if sports-only.
